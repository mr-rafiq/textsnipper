# Code of Conduct

TextSnipper follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project welcoming and respectful for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and security concerns.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Disruptive or hostile behavior

## Enforcement

Report concerns to mohamed-rafiq@outlook.com. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors when needed to protect the project community.
